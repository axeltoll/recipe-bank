Current

0.2.0

The version number in package.json. It brings the first round of work together on top of 0.1.0: the work listed under Being merged below. Parts of it are still on their way into the main branch, and the feature list says which.

Earlier

0.1.0

The first version.

  • A dashboard that filters recipes by ingredient with Has all and Has any, and searches by dish or creator.
  • Recipe pages with the original video embedded, an ingredient checklist, steps with play from here timestamps, and nutrition where the creator states it.
  • Copy a recipe's ingredients as a shopping list.
  • Amounts in the creator's own words, with amount not given where the video does not say, and partial recipes that list their gaps.
  • A queue for new video links from YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.
  • A recipe contract with a validator, and tests on Node's built-in test runner.
  • A read-only static export of the dashboard.
  • Light and dark themes.
Added

This website

A public site for the project in site/, separate from the dashboard.

  • First round: the home page, a showcase built from three of the repository's own recipe files, a self-hosting guide and this changelog.
  • Second round: the dashboard's design tokens, a feature list drawn from one data file with a status on every entry, docs pages for getting started, the recipe format, the analysis and the Recipe Agent, a printable cheat sheet, a contributor page, system fonts in place of web fonts, and every page usable without JavaScript.
Being merged

Being merged

Work that is built on branches and is on its way into the main branch: the redesigned dashboard, the Recipe Agent, the week plan, more filters, serving scaling, the household profile and food budget, a recipe editor and recipe packs. This page does not track each merge. The feature list does: every entry there says whether it is shipped or still being merged.
