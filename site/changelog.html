What is in the current version.

Current

0.1.0

The version in the repository today.

  • A dashboard that filters recipes by ingredient with Has all and Has any, and searches by dish or creator.
  • Recipe pages with the original video embedded, an ingredient checklist, steps with play from here timestamps, and nutrition where the creator states it.
  • Copy a recipe's ingredients as a shopping list.
  • Amounts in the creator's own words, with amount not given where the video does not say, and partial recipes that list their gaps.
  • A queue for new video links from YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.
  • A recipe contract with a validator, and tests on Node's built-in test runner.
  • A read-only static export of the dashboard.
  • Light and dark themes.
Added

This website

A public page for the project in site/, separate from the dashboard: what Recipe Bank is, a showcase built from three of the repository's own recipe files, a self-hosting guide and this changelog.

Planned

What comes next

A week plan with a combined shopping list, more ways to filter, household planning with a food budget, and a Recipe Agent you host yourself. None of it is shipped. The order and the details are in the roadmap.
